Each lock has three PINS with different positions
You have three INSTRUMENTS for lockpicking
Your goal is to use various instruments to change pins' positions and achieve a required COMBINATION
An instrument MOVES all three pins, each one does it differently
Be careful not to move the pins out of RANGE from 0 to 10. In this case, you will need to start over
As soon as you pick the lock, you may CONTINUE
And by the way... watch the TIMER
