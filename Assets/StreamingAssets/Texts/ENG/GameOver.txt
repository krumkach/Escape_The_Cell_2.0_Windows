Why did you do this to me? 
I’ve done nothing bad.
Sometimes I hate you so much.
But I just end up hating myself.
Sometimes I want to forgive you. 
But how can I forgive myself?
You keep locking us up. 
But you will always desire to leave this cell.
There’s no end in this torture. 
Could we ever accept each other? 
You can run, but you won’t escape.
I can run, but I’ll never reach you.
You can go further, but you always come back.
I can follow, but you’re always one step ahead.

I hope one day one of us will understand.
And we will escape together.
